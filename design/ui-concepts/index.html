Wondrous Compass · UI concepts

Six directions for the same three screens (Today, Events, Trading) with the same mock data, so only the design differs. Open each in a browser, resize it to phone width, and click the nav. Countdowns are live.

Concept 1

Cinematic

Like the official site: a big hero, serif display type, a gold call to action.

  • Top navigation, hero banner
  • Best for first impressions and the web landing
Concept 2

Ledger

Dense and fast, in the spirit of GW2BLTC and GW2Efficiency: tables, sparklines, a sidebar.

  • Most information per screen
  • Best for trading and crafting users
Concept 3

Parchment

A light theme drawn from the wiki and the in-game journal: paper, ink, crimson and gold rules.

  • Light by default, with a dark mode toggle (top right) that remembers your choice
  • Most distinct from the current app
Concept 4

Mists HUD

Glass tiles, progress rings, a hero tile for the next boss. WvW and Mists mood.

  • Bento grid, icon rail
  • Most modern, most visual
Concept 5

Sidekick (mobile first)

Built for one thumb: one hero card, big targets, a bottom bar and a vertical agenda instead of a sideways timeline.

  • Opens in a phone frame on desktop
  • Best if most use is on the phone
Concept 6

Refined Gold

The current gold on warm black, with a clearer hierarchy: a left menu, a night-sky hero and an always-on right rail.

  • Smallest change, easiest to build
  • Keeps the look users know

What these drew from

  • guildwars2.com: dark navy, gold buttons, hero art, a multi-level top menu (concept 1).
  • GW2BLTC: compact trading tables with buy and sell columns and item icons at the left (concept 2).
  • GW2Efficiency: account data in dense dashboards (concept 2, concept 6's right rail).
  • The GW2 wiki: light, text-first, easy to scan (concept 3).
  • The in-game Wizard's Vault and Hero panel: progress rings and tracks (concept 4).

All six use mock data and inline SVG icons; the item art is placeholder. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so they need a connection for the intended look.
