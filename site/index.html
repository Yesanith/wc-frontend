Tyria Codex

An unofficial Guild Wars 2 companion for Android, iPhone and the browser.

Your characters, wallet, trading post, crafting, world bosses, the Wizard's Vault and a world map, plus guild events with sign ups, roles and a Discord bot.

Download the latest version

In your browser

There is no iPhone app yet. The whole app also runs in the browser, on any phone or computer.

Open Tyria Codex

iPhone: open it in Safari, tap the share button and choose Add to Home Screen. It then opens on its own, like an app.

The browser version has no notifications, no alerts for world events and no cache on the device, so it loads more slowly. Your API key stays in this browser and is sent only to the Guild Wars 2 servers.
