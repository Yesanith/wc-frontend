Frequently asked questions

What is Tyria Codex?

An unofficial Guild Wars 2 companion: your characters, wallet, trading post, crafting, world bosses, the Wizard's Vault and a world map, plus guild events with sign ups and a Discord bot. It is a fan project, free, with no ads.

Where can I use it?

On Android as an app, and on any phone or computer in a browser at this site's app page. There is no iPhone app yet: open the browser version in Safari, tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen, and it opens like an app. The browser version has no notifications or world event alerts.

Do I need an API key? Which permissions?

The game data comes from your own Guild Wars 2 API key, which you make on account.arena.net. Tick what you want to see: account is always needed, and characters, inventories, wallet, tradingpost and guilds unlock those parts. Without a key you can still use everything that is not about your account.

Is my API key safe?

Your key stays on your device, or in your browser for the browser version, and is sent only to the Guild Wars 2 servers. It is never sent to ours. See the privacy policy. A key can only read, and you can delete it any time on account.arena.net.

Do I need the Tyria Companion account?

No, it is optional. You need it for guild events, the guild information and the Discord bot. You create it by signing in with Discord, and you can delete it any time from Settings or the delete account page.

What happens when I link my Guild Wars 2 account?

The app makes a token that is valid for ten minutes and sends only that, never your key. We record your account name, your guilds and your rank in them. If you lead a guild, the same token lets our servers read what the game shows only to leaders, so your guild's members can see it in the app.

Why can't I see my guild's treasury, stash or log?

The game shows them only to the guild's leader. Members see them here when their leader uses Tyria Companion and lets them. The leader opens the app once, which brings the guild's data, and chooses what members see: any part can be hidden. The treasury, the stash and the log, which show who took what, stay hidden until the leader has confirmed the choices.

It says the leader has not shared this yet.

Either the leader has not opened the app since linking, the game refused a part, or the leader hid it. Ask your leader to open the app and check the eye button on the guild's page.

Who counts as an officer?

The game decides. A rank that may invite, promote or kick, or edit ranks, makes officers, and officers can create and manage events. Nobody is appointed by hand. The app shows the rank name your guild gave, like Raid Lead.

How do I add the Discord bot?

Invite it to your server, then a guild leader who has Manage Server runs /tyria connect with the guild's tag or name in the channel where events should appear. The bot needs to see and write in the channel; for threads, scheduled events, roles and reminders it needs the matching permissions, and /tyria status says what is missing.

What can the bot do?

/event create and /event weekly make events, players sign up with buttons, with their profession and roles. /event report adds an Arcdps report link. /tyria settings sets threads, Discord events and reminders. /tyria notify posts daily achievements, news and game updates. /tyria roles and /tyria rankrole give Discord roles for guild ranks. The bot cannot read messages.

How do Discord roles follow guild ranks?

Run /tyria roles ranks:true. The bot then makes one Discord role for each rank of your guild, with no permissions, and gives it to whoever holds the rank. It starts once the leader's app has brought the ranks; until then the roles of member, officer and leader apply.

Why is something missing in the browser?

The browser version cannot use the phone's notifications, world event alerts or a cache on the device, so it loads more slowly. If something else does not work, tell us.

How do I delete my data?

Settings, then Delete account, or the delete account page. Your profile, linked accounts, memberships, sign ups and the events you created are deleted at once. What the guild's leader brought is deleted when the last leader who uses the app goes.

Is it affiliated with ArenaNet?

No. It is a fan project. Guild Wars 2 and its logos and designs are trademarks of NCSOFT Corporation.

Something is wrong. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub and say what you did and what you saw. A screenshot helps. Never post your API key.
